How to create a video from Google Location History

Use a Google Timeline export to show a trip on an animated map and share the story of your route.

1. Export your Timeline

Start by exporting your location history as a JSON file. Follow the Android and iPhone export guide if you need help finding the setting.

2. Import it into JourneyTrace

Open JourneyTrace and choose the JSON file. Your route appears on the map, along with its distance, dates, and location count.

3. Choose the visual story

  • Fixed: keeps the full route visible.
  • Steady: follows the marker at a stable, useful scale.
  • Dynamic: adjusts framing to the route context.

4. Preview and export

Choose the duration, aspect ratio, and quality. Preview the animation first, then create an MP4 directly in the browser.

Your data stays on your device

JourneyTrace was designed to process Timeline exports locally, without requiring you to upload location history to an external service.
