How to export your Google Timeline data

Export your Google location history and turn the route into an animated travel map without uploading the file to a server.

Privacy first

JourneyTrace processes the export in your browser. Your location data is not uploaded to JourneyTrace.

On Android

  1. Open your Android Settings.
  2. Go to Location → Location services → Timeline.
  3. Select Export Timeline data.
  4. Save the exported JSON file on your device.

On iPhone

  1. Open Google Maps.
  2. Tap your profile picture and open Settings.
  3. Open Personal content.
  4. Select Export Timeline data and save the JSON file.

Create your animated map

  1. Open JourneyTrace.
  2. Select the exported JSON file.
  3. Review your route and choose a camera, format, and duration.
  4. Preview the result or export your video.
